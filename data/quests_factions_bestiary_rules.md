# Quests of Eldervale

## Main Quest: The Starwell Relic

**Objective**: Reach the Moonlit Gate, open it, and retrieve the Starwell Relic before anyone else does — and before the Centennial (12 days away).

**Why it matters**: The Starwell Relic is believed to be a pre-Sundering artifact that recorded the exact moment the moon shattered. It contains the truth about what caused The Sundering. Different factions want it for different reasons.

**Quest Stages**:
1. **Arrival in Thornwall**: Get information, resupply, learn about the gate
2. **The Road to the Gate**: Travel through the Ashfields, face encounters
3. **The Chapel Ruins**: Explore the ruined chapel surrounding the gate
4. **Opening the Gate**: Discover and speak the three conflicting truths
5. **The Vision**: Experience the memory of the night before The Sundering
6. **The Truth**: Learn what really happened — and decide what to do with this knowledge

**Key Clues to Find**:
- Aldren's Journal (in Maren Doss's cellar in Thornwall) — describes the three conflicting truths needed
- The eye sigil rubbing — confirms the gate location
- The Silver Root archives (Finn has access) — hints that the Order's founders knew more

**Possible Endings**:
- Share the truth with the world → begin healing Eldervale
- Destroy the relic → truth stays hidden, world continues as is
- Give relic to Grey Fingers → unknown consequences, Zara's secret revealed
- Give relic to Kael → he destroys the evidence, his secret is safe

**Tags**: quest, main-quest, starwell-relic, moonlit-gate

# Factions of Eldervale

## The Silver Root Order

**Type**: Religious/healing organization
**Goal**: Heal the damage The Sundering caused to the land and people
**Leader**: High Tender Mora Ashveil (in the capital Eastmere)
**Local representative**: Finn Ashroot (party healer)
**Attitude toward party**: Allied — they want the same outcome in theory

**Secret**: The Order's founders witnessed The Sundering. The first High Tender chose to hide the cause to prevent mass panic and retribution against magic users.

**Resources**: Healing supplies, historical archives, safe houses across Verdant Rim, political influence in Eastmere

**Tags**: faction, silver-root-order, healing, religious

---

## The Grey Fingers

**Type**: Thieves' guild / information network
**Goal**: Control of valuable artifacts and information — power through knowledge
**Leader**: Unknown (called only "The Archivist")
**Local representative**: Zara Dusk (secretly)
**Attitude toward party**: Neutral to hostile — they want the relic

**Operations**: Run the Underpath network, sell information, traffic in rare artifacts, have spies in most major settlements

**Secret**: The Archivist already knows what caused The Sundering. They have been suppressing this information for 60 years, selling fragments of the truth to powerful buyers.

**Tags**: faction, grey-fingers, thieves-guild, underground

---

## The Ashfield Wardens

**Type**: Military/protective organization
**Goal**: Keep people out of the Ashfields to prevent casualties
**Leader**: Commander Dael Ironbrook (based in Thornwall)
**Attitude toward party**: Suspicious — they don't want more explorers getting killed

**Resources**: Armed patrols, blockades, knowledge of surface routes through the Ashfields

**Tags**: faction, ashfield-wardens, military, thornwall

# Bestiary

## Moonstone Wraith

**Type**: Undead magical construct
**Danger Level**: Medium
**Found in**: Ashfields (night only), near ruins, near the Moonlit Gate

**Description**: Translucent humanoid figures made of shattered moonstone fragments. They glow with cold blue light. They were once people who were killed in The Sundering and whose souls were trapped in the moonstone debris.

**Behavior**: Passive during the day (dormant in ruins). Aggressive at night. Attracted to active magic use. Avoid open flame.

**Combat Stats**:
- Health: 16
- Attack: Soul Drain (+3, deals 4 damage and reduces max HP by 1 until rest)
- Defense: 12 (immune to non-magical weapons)
- Speed: Slow but relentless

**Weaknesses**: Fire (double damage), bright light (disadvantage on attacks), silver weapons (bypass immunity)

**Loot**: Moonstone shards (valuable, used in dampening rings), soul crystal (rare)

**Tags**: bestiary, moonstone-wraith, undead, ashfields, night

---

## Cave Crawler

**Type**: Giant insect
**Danger Level**: Easy
**Found in**: Underpaths, cave systems

**Description**: Six-legged insect the size of a large dog. Hard shell, poor eyesight, navigates by vibration. Not aggressive unless disturbed or blocking its path.

**Combat Stats**:
- Health: 8
- Attack: Mandible Bite (+2, deals 3 damage)
- Defense: 14 (hard shell)
- Speed: Fast in tunnels

**Weaknesses**: Loud noise (frightens and disorients), attacking legs (bypass shell defense)

**Tags**: bestiary, cave-crawler, insect, underpaths

---

## Echo Guardian

**Type**: Ancient magical construct
**Danger Level**: Very Hard — Boss encounter
**Found in**: Moonlit Gate (summoned if gate opened incorrectly)

**Description**: A massive humanoid figure made of compressed moonstone, standing 12 feet tall. Its eyes glow with the same light as the three moon shards in the sky. It does not speak. It simply protects.

**Combat Stats**:
- Health: 45
- Attack: Moonstone Slam (+6, deals 8 damage, knockback)
- Attack 2: Shard Storm (area, deals 5 damage to all in range)
- Defense: 16
- Special: Immune to magic surges — absorbs magical energy directed at it

**Weaknesses**: The three conflicting truths (speaking them causes it to pause for one round), Starwell Relic (if possessed, Guardian stands down)

**Defeat condition**: Cannot be killed — only banished by speaking the three truths

**Tags**: bestiary, echo-guardian, construct, moonlit-gate, boss

# Homebrew Rules

## Core Mechanics

### Ability Checks
When a character attempts something uncertain, roll 1d20 and add the relevant modifier:
- Result ≥ Difficulty Class (DC): Success
- Result < DC: Failure
- Result = DC: Partial success (succeed with a cost)

**Standard DCs**:
- Easy: 8
- Medium: 12
- Hard: 16
- Very Hard: 20

### Combat

**Initiative**: Each combatant rolls 1d20 at start of combat. Highest goes first.

**Attack Roll**: Roll 1d20 + attack modifier vs target's Defense score
- Hit: Roll damage dice
- Miss: No damage

**Health and Death**:
- Reach 0 HP: Unconscious and dying
- Receive healing while dying: Stabilize at 1 HP
- Three failed death saves (1d20 < 10): Dead

### Rest
- **Short rest** (1 hour): Recover 1d6 HP, refresh minor abilities
- **Long rest** (8 hours): Recover full HP, refresh all abilities, remove exhaustion

### Magic Surge (Post-Sundering Rule)
Whenever a spell is cast in the Ashfields, roll 1d20:
- 1-7: Surge — spell has uncontrolled amplified effect (Game Master decides)
- 8-20: Normal effect

Outside Ashfields, surge only on a roll of 1.
