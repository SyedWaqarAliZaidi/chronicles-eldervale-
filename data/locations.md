# Locations of Eldervale

## Location: The Moonlit Gate

**Type**: Ancient ruin — stone archway
**Region**: Northern Ashfields, half a day's walk from Thornwall village
**Difficulty**: High danger zone

**Summary**:
The Moonlit Gate is a massive silver stone archway buried within the ruins of an old chapel. It stands 30 feet tall and is carved with eye-shaped sigils along its frame. The gate glows faintly under moonlight. It is believed to be the entrance to the vault containing the Starwell Relic.

**Known Facts** (players can discover):
- The gate only activates under moonlight
- Three conflicting truths must be spoken by three different people to open it
- The eye sigil on the gate matches a symbol in the missing wizard Aldren's journal
- The Silver Root Order once used this chapel for pilgrimages before The Sundering

**Secrets** (Game Master only):
- The gate does not open to a vault. It opens to a memory — a vision of Eldervale the night before The Sundering
- Opening the gate without the Starwell Relic summons the Echo Guardian, a construct made of shattered moonstone
- The three conflicting truths must be genuine beliefs, not lies — the gate can detect deception

**Encounters**:
- Moonstone Wraiths patrol at night (difficulty: medium)
- Echo Guardian if gate opened incorrectly (difficulty: very hard)
- Rival agent Kael Thorn may be camped nearby

**Related Entities**: Starwell Relic, Silver Root Order, Kael Thorn, Aldren's Journal
**Tags**: location, moonlit-gate, ruins, chapel, ashfields, gate, relic

## Location: Thornwall Village

**Type**: Settlement
**Region**: Edge of the Verdant Rim, bordering the Ashfields
**Population**: ~400 survivors and settlers

**Summary**:
A small fortified village built from the stones of an old watchtower. Thornwall is the last safe settlement before entering the Ashfields. It serves as a waypoint for ruin explorers and traders using the Underpaths.

**Known Facts**:
- Has a working inn called The Three Shards (named after the moon fragments)
- The village elder, Maren Doss, has maps of the Ashfields from her late husband's expeditions
- A Silver Root Order healer named Brother Caln runs the local infirmary
- Strange lights have been seen coming from the Moonlit Gate direction for the past week

**Secrets**:
- Maren Doss knows where Aldren's Journal is hidden — in her cellar — but won't reveal this easily
- The village has a secret entrance to the Underpaths behind the mill

**Services Available**:
- Inn (rest, remove exhaustion)
- Infirmary (heal minor wounds, buy healing herbs)
- Blacksmith (basic weapon repair)
- General store (torches, rope, rations, basic supplies)

**Related Entities**: Maren Doss, Brother Caln, Aldren's Journal, Underpaths
**Tags**: location, thornwall, village, settlement, verdant-rim, safe-zone

## Location: The Ashfields

**Type**: Wasteland region
**Region**: Central Eldervale
**Danger Level**: Extreme

**Summary**:
The grey wasteland at the heart of Eldervale. Caused by the magical fallout of The Sundering, the Ashfields are covered in grey dust and the ruins of the old kingdom. Magic is highly unstable here — surge chance increases to 35%. Strange phenomena occur: time echoes (glimpses of the past), floating debris from The Sundering, and corrupted creatures.

**Known Facts**:
- The ruins of the old capital Solmere lie at the center
- Moonstone Wraiths are common after dark
- The magnetic field is disrupted — compasses spin randomly
- Some ruins still have functioning pre-Sundering magical traps

**Secrets**:
- The grey ash is actually crystallized magical energy — it can be harvested but is unstable
- The time echoes are not random — they are memories the land is trying to show travelers
- There is a network of pre-Sundering underground passages beneath the Ashfields that are mostly intact

**Hazards**:
- Magic surge (35% chance on any spell)
- Disorientation (navigation checks required every hour)
- Moonstone Wraith attacks (night only)
- Unstable ground (some areas collapse into underground ruins)

**Related Entities**: Solmere ruins, Moonstone Wraiths, Moonlit Gate
**Tags**: location, ashfields, wasteland, dangerous, central-eldervale, ruins

## Location: The Underpaths

**Type**: Underground tunnel network
**Region**: Beneath the Verdant Rim and Ashfields
**Danger Level**: Medium

**Summary**:
A vast network of natural caves and carved tunnels that run beneath most of Eldervale. Used by merchants, refugees, and thieves' guild couriers to travel safely. The tunnels avoid most surface dangers but have their own threats.

**Known Facts**:
- The Thornwall entrance is behind the mill (known to locals)
- Bioluminescent fungi light most major passages naturally
- The guild of cartographers in Eastmere sells maps of the known sections
- Travel time beneath the Ashfields is 6 hours vs 12 hours on the surface

**Secrets**:
- The thieves' guild (called The Grey Fingers) controls most of the Underpath network
- There is an Underpath entrance directly beneath the Moonlit Gate chapel — bypassing the surface approach entirely
- Ancient pre-Sundering inscriptions line the deepest tunnels — they are a map to something

**Hazards**:
- Cave crawlers (insect creatures, difficulty: easy)
- Grey Fingers toll collectors (may demand payment or information)
- Occasional cave-ins near the Ashfield sections

**Related Entities**: Grey Fingers, Thornwall Village, Moonlit Gate
**Tags**: location, underpaths, tunnels, underground, travel, grey-fingers
