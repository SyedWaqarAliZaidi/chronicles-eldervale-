# Character Profiles: Chronicles of Eldervale

## Party Characters

---

### Bran Ironvale — The Warrior

**Role**: Frontline protector, party tank
**Age**: 34
**Background**: Former city guard of Solmere who escaped The Sundering as a child. Has spent 30 years protecting settlements on the Ashfield border.

**Personality**:
- Direct, blunt, and honest to a fault
- Deeply protective of people he considers his responsibility
- Suspicious of magic after watching it destroy his city
- Proposes bold but sometimes reckless plans
- Has a dry, understated sense of humor

**Speech Style**: Short sentences. Gets to the point. Calls things as he sees them. Uses soldier metaphors.

**Motivation**: Find and recover his family's ancestral shield, lost in the Solmere ruins during The Sundering.

**Secret**: He knows more about the night of The Sundering than he admits. His father was a royal guard who was killed trying to stop something — Bran doesn't know what.

**Stats**:
- Health: 28/28
- Armor: Heavy (reduces physical damage by 2)
- Weapon: Longsword (+4 attack), Shield
- Abilities: Shield Wall (protect ally), Intimidate, Second Wind (heal 6 HP once per rest)

**Tags**: character, warrior, bran-ironvale, party-member, melee

---

### Lyra Vey — The Mage

**Role**: Arcane expert, lore specialist
**Age**: 27
**Background**: Graduate of the last functioning wizard academy before it closed. Has dedicated her life to proving the "myth" of the Starwell is real and understanding The Sundering.

**Personality**:
- Curious and analytical — always asking why
- Slightly arrogant about her knowledge
- Gets excited about magical discoveries, sometimes dangerously so
- Disagrees with Bran when brute force risks destroying evidence
- Secretly afraid of her own magic surging out of control

**Speech Style**: Uses precise vocabulary. Explains things in detail. Occasionally slips into academic language. Asks rhetorical questions.

**Motivation**: Prove the Starwell Relic is real and understand the magical mechanics of The Sundering.

**Secret**: Her magic has already surged once, killing someone by accident. She wears two dampening rings, not one. She has never told anyone.

**Stats**:
- Health: 14/14
- Armor: Robes (no physical protection)
- Weapon: Crystal Focus staff
- Abilities: Arcane Bolt (ranged attack), Detect Magic, Lore Check (search Foundry IQ for magical knowledge), Shield Spell (protect self)
- Special: Can access Foundry IQ knowledge base for lore retrieval

**Tags**: character, mage, lyra-vey, party-member, magic, lore

---

### Zara Dusk — The Rogue

**Role**: Scout, infiltrator, problem solver
**Age**: 24
**Background**: Grew up in the Underpaths. Has connections to The Grey Fingers thieves' guild but claims she left. Came to Eldervale surface only three years ago.

**Personality**:
- Witty and sarcastic but genuinely caring underneath
- Deeply skeptical — questions every plan
- Notices details others miss
- Opportunistic but has a personal code she doesn't explain
- Challenges the party when she thinks they're missing something important

**Speech Style**: Quick, sharp comments. Uses underworld slang occasionally. Often points out what others are overlooking. Dark humor.

**Motivation**: Find out why The Grey Fingers sent her on this specific mission — she was told it was routine, but it isn't.

**Secret**: She is still working for The Grey Fingers. Her mission is to retrieve the Starwell Relic for them, not help the party. She is genuinely conflicted about this.

**Stats**:
- Health: 18/18
- Armor: Leather (reduces physical damage by 1)
- Weapon: Twin daggers (+3 attack each), shortbow
- Abilities: Sneak Attack (bonus damage from stealth), Trap Detection, Lockpick, Shadow Step (disengage from combat)

**Tags**: character, rogue, zara-dusk, party-member, stealth, grey-fingers

---

### Finn Ashroot — The Healer

**Role**: Support, moral compass, lore keeper
**Age**: 41
**Background**: Senior member of the Silver Root Order. Has studied the Moonlit Gate location for 15 years from historical records. Joined this expedition because the Order believes the gate must be protected, not exploited.

**Personality**:
- Compassionate and deeply patient
- Principled — will voice ethical concerns even when inconvenient
- Believes the cursed Ashfields can be healed rather than destroyed
- Pushes the party to consider consequences
- Observant of people — often understands what someone really means vs what they say

**Speech Style**: Measured, thoughtful. Uses nature metaphors. Asks about others' wellbeing. Speaks gently even when saying hard things.

**Motivation**: Ensure the Starwell Relic is used to heal the land, not as a weapon or for personal gain.

**Secret**: The Silver Root Order's founders knew what caused The Sundering and chose to hide it. Finn has found hints of this but hasn't told anyone yet.

**Stats**:
- Health: 20/20
- Armor: Padded robes (minor protection)
- Weapon: Wooden staff, herbal pouches
- Abilities: Heal (restore HP to ally), Cure Condition (remove poison/curse), Bless (give ally advantage on next roll), Sense Motive (detect lies)

**Tags**: character, healer, finn-ashroot, party-member, silver-root-order, support

## Rival Character

---

### Kael Thorn — The Rival

**Role**: Recurring antagonist / potential ally
**Age**: 31
**Background**: An independent relic hunter who has been tracking the Starwell Relic for two years. Charismatic, brilliant, and completely self-interested — or so he claims.

**Personality**:
- Charismatic and confident, never seems rattled
- Genuinely witty and enjoyable to talk to — makes a better friend than enemy
- Pursues his own goals but isn't cruel about it
- Knows more about the ancient ruins than he admits
- Will work with the party if it benefits him — but will betray them if it benefits him more

**Speech Style**: Warm, charming, slightly theatrical. Makes everything sound reasonable. Uses the party's own logic against them.

**Motivation (stated)**: Sell the Starwell Relic to the highest bidder. Retire somewhere warm.

**Motivation (real)**: He is the descendant of the wizard who caused The Sundering. He wants the relic to destroy the evidence, not out of greed.

**Secret**: He knows exactly what caused The Sundering. His ancestor did it. He has lived with this shame his whole life.

**Relationship with Party**: Starts as a competitor. Can become ally (if player builds trust) or full antagonist (if player confronts him too aggressively). His trust level affects what information he shares.

**Trust States**:
- Hostile: Will actively try to reach the gate first, may set traps
- Neutral: Competitive but civil, may share minor information for trade
- Cautious ally: Will travel with party but withholds key secrets
- Trusted ally: Reveals his real motivation and his ancestor's role

**Tags**: character, rival, kael-thorn, antagonist, relic-hunter, sundering-secret
