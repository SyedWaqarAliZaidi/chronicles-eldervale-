# World Overview: Eldervale

## Setting
Eldervale is a high-fantasy realm where the moon shattered exactly 100 years ago in an event known as "The Sundering." No one knows what caused it. The three shards of the broken moon hang permanently in the sky, visible day and night, and have caused magic to become unstable and unpredictable across the land.

## Tone
Dark, mysterious, hopeful. The world is broken but people are rebuilding. Magic is feared but necessary. Ancient ruins hide the truth about The Sundering.

## Geography
- **The Ashfields**: A vast grey wasteland at the center of Eldervale, where magic is most unstable. The ruins of the old capital city lie here.
- **The Verdant Rim**: Lush forests and farmlands surrounding the Ashfields. Most surviving settlements are here.
- **The Shattered Coast**: Eastern coastline where moonfall debris crashed into the ocean, creating glowing sea caves.
- **The Northern Peaks**: Mountain range where the old wizard academies once stood. Now abandoned and dangerous.
- **The Underpaths**: A network of underground tunnels used by merchants and refugees to avoid surface dangers.

## Laws of Magic (Post-Sundering)
- Magic still works but has a 15% chance of "surging" — producing an amplified, uncontrolled effect
- Healing magic is the most stable form remaining
- Elemental magic is the most volatile
- Ancient magic (pre-Sundering) is completely unpredictable
- Magic users wear "dampening rings" to reduce surge risk

## The Central Conflict
The Starwell Relic — an ancient artifact believed to be the key to understanding The Sundering — has been located in the ruins beneath the Moonlit Gate. Multiple factions want it for different reasons. The player's party must reach it first.

## Calendar
- 400 days per year, 10 months of 40 days each
- Current year: Year 100 Post-Sundering (the centennial)
- The anniversary of The Sundering is in 12 days — believed to be when the relic becomes active

## Key Historical Events
- **Year 0**: The Sundering — the moon shatters, the old kingdom collapses overnight
- **Year 1-20**: The Dark Decades — chaos, famine, magical storms
- **Year 21-60**: The Rebuilding — new settlements form, the Silver Root Order is founded
- **Year 61-99**: The Quiet Century — relative stability, ruins exploration begins
- **Year 100 (now)**: The Centennial — something is awakening in the old ruins
